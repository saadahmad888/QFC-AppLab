Experience Growth, Innovation

The Qatar Financial Centre (QFC) is an onshore business and financial centre located in Doha, established by Law No. 7 of 2005 of the State of Qatar. A world-class platform for companies, QFC offers its own legal, regulatory, tax and business environment, contributing to the decisive economic development and diversification prescribed by Qatar National Vision 2030

Supported by QFC’s broad range of legal and regulatory services, regulated and non-regulated

Vision

Transforming Doha into a leading global financial and commercial capital.

Mission

Our mission is to drive economic development and diversification by providing a world-class, commercial, legal and regulatory environment.

Our Core Values

Integrity, innovation, collaboration, and excellence drive everything we do.

QFCA

Quality

We are committed to excellence in every operation and consistently deliver outstanding results with confidence.

Future Focus

We anticipate, adapt to, and capitalise on evolving business landscapes. We embrace learning and foster positive transformation.

Customer Focus

We are dedicated to prioritising the needs, satisfaction, and success of our customers. We are in business together.

Authenticity

We are genuine, transparent, and true to our purpose. Together, we create strategic solutions.

A Workplace Built for People

QFC is governed by three independent organisations that work together to promote transparency, compliance and business confidence. Each institution plays a critical role in creating a global business centre and environment.

Collaborative Spirit

At QFC, we believe that working together leads to greater success. Our open and inclusive environment encourages teams to share ideas and solve challenges collectively.

Diversity & Inclusion

Our community is enriched by people from different cultures and backgrounds. We foster respect and inclusion, ensuring everyone’s voice is heard and valued.

Learning & Growth

Continuous development is key. QFC offers training, mentorship, and access to global resources so individuals can sharpen their skills and advance their careers.

Wellbeing & Balance

We care about the whole person. Wellness initiatives, flexible work arrangements, and support programs ensure a healthy balance between work and personal life.

Innovation-Driven Mindset

Creativity and experimentation are encouraged. We support forward-thinking approaches that inspire new solutions and help businesses stay ahead in a fast-changing world.

Career & Opportunity 

Join a fast-growing financial center: QFC’s ecosystem is evolving at pace, creating new possibilities every day. 

  • Contribute to meaningful work across regulation, licensing, innovation, and asset management — all under one roof.
  • Gain exposure to local and regional markets while connecting with global financial flows; QFC’s strategic position offers worldwide reach from Doha. 
  • Shape the future by working on emerging priorities such as digital assets, sustainability-linked finance, and fintech regulation.

Internships & Student Engagement
  • Summer and year-round programs designed to give you real responsibilities.
  • Work on projects that matter — no busy work.
  • Build your network through exposure to regulators, industry leaders
Qatari Development Program

QFC is committed to nurturing national talent through a dedicated development track aimed at helping Qatari professionals grow into the future leaders of the organisation. The program includes targeted learning, coaching, and strategic assignments.

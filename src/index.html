If your account is managed by your organization, you won't see a "Billing" tab because billing is handled centrally by your Primary Owner or administrator.To confirm your subscription details, use these methods:Check the Profile Menu: Click your name or initials in the bottom-left corner of claude.ai. On many managed accounts, your current plan name (e.g., "Team" or "Enterprise") will appear directly under your name in that menu.Toggle Accounts: If you have both a personal and a work account associated with the same email, click your initials to see if you can switch between them. One will likely be your personal "Free" or "Pro" account, and the other will be your organization's plan.Check for Enterprise Features: Look for features like Projects or "Claude for Work" settings. If you can create shared project folders for your team, you are on a Team or Enterprise plan.Use the /usage Command: If you use Claude Code (the terminal version), type /usage or /stats. This command bypasses the web UI and will display your plan's usage limits directly.Since you can't access billing, you should contact your organization's IT department or Primary Owner for specific details regarding your seat's limits and features.Would you like to know how to contact support if you're having trouble with your organizational access?

QFC is a leading onshore business and financial centre located in Doha, committed to promoting Qatar as a major business and commercial hub at the crossroads between East and West.

We invite businesses, from start-ups to large corporations seeking to expand to the region, to take advantage of a robust legal and regulatory environment aligned with international best practice, covering both regulated and non-regulated activities. With QFC, you can accelerate your business growth in Qatar and beyond.

QFC Tech Circle

  1. Home
  2. Opening with QFC
  3. QFC Tech

Empowering Qatars Digital and FinTech Innovation

The QFC Tech Circle, located on the 9th Floor of QFC Tower 1, is a flexible co-working space that supports early-stage digital and FinTech companies from Qatar and abroad to operate with ease and at reduced cost.

As a thought-leader in the digital and FinTech sectors, QFC facilitates collaboration, networking and discussion among the tech community.

The platform supports the State of Qatar’s strategic initiatives in the digital innovation space, alongside Qatar Development Bank’s (QDB) Digital Incubation Centre and Qatar Fintech Hub, Qatar Smart Program’s (TASMU) Innovation Lab, and Qatar SportsTech.

Services

The QFC Tech Circle offers a suite of services designed to help early-stage digital and FinTech companies grow efficiently.

Registered Address

Clients can use the QFC Tech Circle as their registered address/virtual office (for a period of 6–18 months, depending on eligibility).

Co-working Space
  • Company receives 2 passes for use of the co-working space
  • Guest/visitor passes available upon request (meetings etc.)
Tech Talks

Access to monthly QFC Tech Talk Series

Client Support

Access to a dedicated client support team

Floor Manager

Dedicated floor manager to support your operations
